# Issue #498: test_mutmut_forksafe leaks a deadlocked process on every run, and its 'clean' case flakes

## What happened

On 2026-10-19 there were **485 hung `python -c` processes** on the dev machine, about 300 MB RSS. They were spread across a dozen worktrees and up to about 10 days old, each sleeping at 0% CPU with PPID 1. Every one was running `_forks_cleanly`'s probe program from `tests/unit/test_mutmut_forksafe.py`. They were killed by hand.

## Why: one leak per unit-suite run, by design

`test_one_polars_operation_poisons_the_fork` asserts that the forked child **hangs**: polars' rayon pool is poisoned across `fork()`. `_forks_cleanly` runs the probe with `subprocess.run(..., timeout=_TIMEOUT_S)`, which on timeout kills only the direct child, i.e. the probe's parent. The deadlocked grandchild is re-parented to init and never exits.

So every `just test` / `just ci`, in every worktree, leaks one process. Four new orphans appeared within 10 minutes of four suite runs.

## Second symptom: `test_importing_polars_leaves_the_fork_clean` flakes

This test failed once in `just ci` on `feat/492-stage2-ref`. Nothing it touches changed there, and it passed on a rerun. The machine was under heavy load at the time: a full bundle build plus xdist on all 10 cores. A clean child normally finishes in under a second against a 10 s timeout.

`_forks_cleanly` cannot tell a slow child from a hung one (it says so, deliberately), so the failure is one of two things:
- a load-induced timeout, or
- the "importing polars leaves fork clean" fact holding only most of the time, which would undermine `tools/mutmut_forksafe.py`'s preload.

The outputs here can't distinguish them.

## Fix options

1. **Stop the leak.** Run the probe with `start_new_session=True` and, on `TimeoutExpired`, `os.killpg(proc.pid, SIGKILL)` the whole group, so the hung grandchild dies with its parent. Add a test asserting no probe process survives the poisoning case. Per the "a guard has to stay able to fail" rules, it has to be watched failing against the current helper first.
2. **Separate slow from hung** in the clean case. For example, report whether the grandchild exited or is still in the process group at the timeout, so a flake under load reads differently from a real poisoning. Then measure the clean case's hang rate at rest (many runs, idle machine) before deciding whether fact (2) still holds.
