## Comments

### 2026-08-05

Closed by !207. Final: **5 → 1** complexity suppressions in `src/`.

The value of the re-triage was not the count — it was that the original triage was wrong on three of five. I had recorded `_emit_predicate` *and the two `read`s* as "dispatch-shaped, inherent". Measured fresh, only `_emit_predicate` is dispatch. The `read`s' entire branch count was five mechanical `for x in xs: index_artefact(...)` loops, which one helper collapses; that also cleaned **ml21**, never on this list because it sat one artefact type under the threshold.

That misfiling has the same root cause recorded when #209 closed: pattern-matching a new case onto a previous one ("long function, many steps" → "must be dispatch, like the other one") instead of judging it fresh. Two issues in a row. The corrective that worked both times was mechanical — re-measure every item before touching it, and never carry a prior verdict forward.

Also worth recording: `write_text_format` was the one item where the linter was pointing at a real defect risk rather than a shape. Eleven `if x is not None: el.set(...)` statements are a table; a `Facet` field added and not mirrored there is silent data loss on write that XSD validation cannot catch. Worth doing at any complexity score.

`_emit_predicate` keeps its suppression and now documents why — dead parameters on eight of eleven emitters, or SQL inside the DSL structs. It is the only complexity suppression left, and the only one that ever explained itself.
