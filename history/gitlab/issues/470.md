## What

- **`ReportingTaxonomy`:** an item scheme of `ReportingCategory`s, each grouping the dataflows and metadataflows a reporting framework asks for. It exists in 2.1, 3.0 and 3.1.
- **`ReportingTaxonomyMap`** (3.x): maps one reporting taxonomy onto another, like the other scheme maps. 2.1 holds the equivalent inside a `StructureSet`, as it does the maps sdmxlib already models (#421).

## Now

Both are refused on read by name.

sdmx1 models `ReportingTaxonomy` (2.1 only). pysdmx models neither, and nobody models the map.

## Done when

The milestone's checklist holds for both. The map reuses the generic `_read_item_scheme_map` and the `<map>@<set>` id rule that `OrganisationSchemeMap` (#448) uses.
