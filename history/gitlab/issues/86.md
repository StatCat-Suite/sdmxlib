## Plan
- [ ] Create \`src/sdmxlib/formats/sdmx_ml/_common.py\` (or \`_helpers.py\`) parameterised by namespace constants \`(COM, STR, MES)\`.
- [ ] Move the byte-identical helpers first; keep the version-specific call sites as thin wrappers.
- [ ] Then attack the near-duplicates with version flags or small extension hooks (the \`MeasureDimension\` reader can live in 2.1 only; everything else converges).
- [ ] Consider whether \`_resolve_agency\` also moves to a higher-up shared helper used by \`sdmx_json/reader.py\`.
