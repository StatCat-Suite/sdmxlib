# Issue #457: FMR as an external oracle: our output read and served back by an independent implementation

## Problem

Several constructs are tested only against ourselves: our writer, then our reader, then the vendored XSD. That shows self-consistency, not interoperability. No swept registry serves these:

- MetadataProvisionAgreements and MetadataProviderSchemes;
- OrganisationSchemeMaps;
- 2.1 StructureSets;
- MeasureDimension (UNSD only);
- codelist extensions with wildcards;
- the synthetic 2.1 MSD reporting layer.

If we and the XSD share a misreading, nothing in the suite can see it. That's the `is_in("CA")` lesson from CLAUDE.md, applied to formats.

## Proposal

Use an independent implementation as an oracle. The Fusion Metadata Registry is the reference 3.x implementation, and pysdmx and sdmx1 are secondary checks.

1. Write the maximal fixture and the version shapes in SDMX-ML 2.1, 3.0, 3.1 and SDMX-JSON.
2. Load each into FMR (a local instance), then fetch it back in every format.
3. Diff what comes back against what we sent, through our reader, and classify each difference as:
   - our bug;
   - FMR's bug or deviation (documented);
   - a declared gap that we and FMR agree on.
4. Record the result as a fixture-backed test that replays cassettes, so `just ci` keeps it without the network.

## Done when

Every construct in the maximal fixture has been through FMR, and every difference is classified.

## Comments

### 2026-10-17

Done: FMR 12.4.2 covers SDMX-ML 2.1 and 3.0 (!618), and pysdmx 1.20.0 covers 3.1 (!619). Both are replayed in ci from recordings, through one comparison in `tests/support/interop.py`. Each external difference is either a declared adjustment or a classified rule, and the ledgers are checked in both directions. sdmx1 was not added as a third oracle, and the FMR/pysdmx defects the ledgers record are not reported upstream for now.
