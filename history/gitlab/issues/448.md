## Today

`OrganisationSchemeMap` is a maintainable in SDMX-ML 2.1 (inside a `StructureSet`), in SDMX-ML 3.0 and 3.1 (`OrganisationSchemeMaps`), and in SDMX-JSON 2.0 (`organisationSchemeMaps`). The model has no class for it, so:

- **3.x and SDMX-JSON:** each one is refused as an unmodelled artefact (`_UNMODELLED`, and `unread_sections` for JSON).
- **2.1:** its `StructureSet` is kept, and the map is reported as a `ReadGap` (`str:StructureSet/str:OrganisationSchemeMap` in `sdmx_ml/_losses.py`).

## Proposal

Model it the way `ConceptSchemeMap` and `CategorySchemeMap` are modelled: `source`/`target` refs to organisation schemes, plus `maps: tuple[ItemMap, ...]` of `OrganisationMap` pairs. This means:

- reader and writer for 2.1, 3.0, 3.1 and SDMX-JSON;
- a storage table and a schema bump;
- the maximal fixture;
- deleting the 2.1 `LOSSES` row, which turns its strict xfail into an XPASS;
- removing it from `_UNMODELLED`.

## Staying as declared losses (not in scope)

- **`ReportingTaxonomyMap`**: it maps `ReportingTaxonomy`, which is unmodelled, as #422's out-of-scope list notes. It stays a reported 2.1 loss and a refused 3.x artefact until `ReportingTaxonomy` is modelled.
- **`HybridCodelistMap`**: this is 2.1-only and maps codes between codelists and hierarchical codelists. It stays a reported loss. One possible later step is to read a map whose two ends are both codelists as a `RepresentationMap`.
