## Comments

### 2026-06-09

Resolved in v0.34.0 — see the registry-methods refactor.

- Free `sl.default_hierarchy` removed. Migrate:
  - to `reg.unique_hierarchy(df, dim_id)` for the at-most-one accessor (raises `AmbiguousHierarchyError` on >1 instead of bare `ValueError`)
  - to `reg.applicable_hierarchies(df, dim_id)` for IM-canonical enumeration of all bound Hierarchies
- `sl.validate_binding` similarly moved to `reg.validate_binding(binding)`.
- The 'raise on >1' behaviour was stricter than the SDMX 3.x IM; the new `applicable_hierarchies` is the canonical many-case answer.
- O(1) inbound-Ref index follow-up tracked in #138.
