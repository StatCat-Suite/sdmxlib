## Measured

Writing BIS's 27 DSDs as SDMX-ML 2.1 went from **7.1 ms to 16.5 ms (+131%)** across milestone 20, while the 3.0 write is flat (4.7 ms to 4.8 ms). pysdmx writes the same DSDs as 2.1 in 4 ms. With validation on (the default), the write takes 19 ms.

## Lead

Not profiled yet. Because the 3.0 write is flat, the cost is likely in the 2.1 projection added by #421, for example:
- `with_derived_targets`;
- the provider-scheme split;
- the measure and attribute conversions;
- the `gaps` checks.

These may run over the whole message even when nothing in it needs projecting.

## Done when

The cause is named by profile plus A/B. The 2.1 DSD write is back near its pre-M20 time, with 2.1 output unchanged, and a count guard is in place.
