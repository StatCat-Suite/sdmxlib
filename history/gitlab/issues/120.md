## State

\`src/sdmxlib/formats/sdmx_ml31/reader.py\` — **51% line coverage** (223 of 457 lines untested).

## Why

3.1 was added in MR !77 as a fork of the 3.0 reader with only the documented divergences (Categorisation \`@version\` dropped, DataConstraint \`@role\` + ReleaseCalendar dropped) overridden. Only one 3.1 fixture exists today (\`tests/fixtures/sdmx_ml31/minimal.xml\`) — it exercises a thin slice. The bulk of read paths (DSDs with all component variants, Hierarchies, MetadataStructures, StructureSets, mapping artefacts) are inherited from 3.0 but never exercised on the 3.1 namespace.

Uncovered line clusters span:
- Lines 95–305: most major artefact read branches
- Lines 471–718: scheme/map readers
- Lines 727–961: hierarchy / metadata structure paths

## Proposed work

Port the 3.0 fixtures we trust (\`complete.xml\`, \`with_groups.xml\`, MSD/Metadataflow fixtures) to 3.1 namespaces with the three documented divergences applied. Add to \`_FIXTURE_NAMES\` in the existing \`test_sdmx_ml31_writer.py\` test (which doubles as a round-trip reader exerciser).

Estimated payoff: ~40 percentage points of coverage closed; surfaces any latent divergences from 3.0 we haven't documented.

## Acceptance

- \`just coverage\` reports \`sdmx_ml31/reader.py\` ≥ 85%.
- Each ported fixture XSD-validates against the vendored v3.1.0 schemas.
- Round-trip tests pass on every fixture.
