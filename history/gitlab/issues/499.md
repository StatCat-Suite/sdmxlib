## 1. Per-module baselines for the rest of the query compilers

`_expr.py` (669 statements), `_joins.py` (109) and `_common.py` (79) joined `only_mutate` in !329 (`pyproject.toml`) and have never been run. `_compile.py` has baselines (1,290 mutants; whole-suite 87.4% before triage); the other three have none.

`_expr.py` is the largest module in the query package, and probably a richer seam than `_compile.py`'s tail.

- Run each with `just mutate-module`, under mutmut 3.8 with the `CreditingRunner` (installment 9, `forkserver_warmup = "none"`). Numbers from 3.7 are not comparable: the runner change moved `gaps.py` from 317 to 501 kills.
- Record killed / survived / timeout / wall per module in `mutation-evidence.md`.
- Sample survivors in a fresh process before trusting the runner's verdicts.

## 2. Full-scope wall-clock

`just mutate` over the whole configured scope has not been timed since the scope changed. The old ~4.5 h figure for 923 statements is void, because it was dominated by the mis-booked timeouts of #303/#314. The scope has also grown since #304: #458 added the readers and writers.

- Time one full run on an otherwise idle machine, and record the scope size (`just mutate-scope`) beside the number.
