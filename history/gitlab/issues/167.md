**Tier 1 — safe, model-core.** From the `Any` audit (bonus finding).

`ItemList[T]` is declared with **no upper bound**, yet the body needs `item.id`
— hence the `getattr(item, "id", None)` + `cast("str", item.id)  # pyright:
ignore[reportAttributeAccessIssue]` dance at `model/collections.py:82-84,134`.
Its sibling `ArtefactList[T: Maintainable]` is bounded and needs none of this.

**Fix:** add a one-property `HasId` Protocol (`id: str`) and declare
`class ItemList[T: HasId]`. Delete the cast + the `# pyright: ignore` and the
defensive `getattr`.

**Acceptance:** cast + ignore gone; `.ids()`/`__init__`/`to_dict` checked; every
`ItemList` element type (Code, Dimension, Concept, …) satisfies `HasId`; `just
ci` green; existing tests pass.
**Risk:** low.
