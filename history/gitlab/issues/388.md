Direction (2) is measured and does not work — recorded here so it is not re-tried.

Declaring the sidecar's key so DuckDB elides the join itself:

- `UNIQUE INDEX` on `(REF_AREA, GEO_LEVEL, ALT_GEO_CODE)` — the hash join stays in the plan and the whole-lake count stays at **2612 ms**. Unchanged.
- `PRIMARY KEY` — not expressible: `ALT_GEO_CODE` is nullable in `geography_attributes` on the real bundle, so the constraint is refused at insert.

So whatever closes the *referenced* case, it is not a constraint on the sidecar.

The sidecar **is** unique on its key there — 79,176 rows, 79,176 distinct keys — which is the soundness condition (1) relies on and the one DuckDB will not assume.
