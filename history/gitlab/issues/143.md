## Comments

### 2026-08-20

**Closing — implemented.**

`_delete_artefact_cascade` now returns immediately when the artefact is not
stored, which on a fresh materialisation is every artefact. From its docstring:

> **Returns immediately when the artefact is not stored**, which on a fresh
> materialisation is every artefact. […] Measured on ECB's dataflow catalogue
> (183 artefacts), skipping them took the write from 8.5 ms to 3.1 ms per
> artefact (#317).

That is this issue's step 2, landed under #317 rather than under this number.

Worth recording what the measurement found, because it was not what this issue
predicted: two thirds of the saving was not the `DELETE`s. Each binds `urn` as a
parameter, and DuckDB probes every parameter value for a pandas null with a lazy
`import pandas` — absent here and uncached, so each probe re-walks `sys.path`.
6,588 deletes carried ~21,500 of those.

Steps 3 (#144) and 4 (#145) remain open and still apply to the **re-ingest**
path, which this change does not touch.
