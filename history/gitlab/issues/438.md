## Summary

A component's `enumeration_format` (the facet beside an enumerated representation: `String`, min/max length) is dropped by `LocalRegistry` on `add` → `get`.

Found by the registry sweep (!516) at **every** registry: e.g. `BIS:BIS_CBPOL(1.0)` FREQ has `representation=Ref(CL_FREQ)` and `enumeration_format=Facet(type='String', min_length=1, max_length=1)`; read back, `enumeration_format` is `None`. ECB 92 DSDs, BIS 26, FMR 19, OECD 4.

## Root cause

`_representation_columns` (`storage/writers.py:849`, enumeration arm `:872`) writes `["enumeration", urn, None, …]`: the facet columns are always NULL there, and nothing in `storage/` reads or writes `enumeration_format`. The DSD reader (`storage/readers.py:569`, facet branch near `:711-725`) builds a `Facet` only when `repr_kind == 'facet'`. `Measure` and `MetadataAttribute` carry the field too.

## Fix direction

No DDL change: in the enumeration arm, fill the (otherwise NULL) facet columns from `enumeration_format`, and have the readers rebuild it when `repr_kind == 'enumeration'` and any of them is set. Extend the facet-group guard test to cover it.
