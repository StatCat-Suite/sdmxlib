## What

Two organisation schemes sdmxlib doesn't model, both in SDMX-ML 2.1 and 3.x and SDMX-JSON:

- **`DataConsumerScheme`** (`DataConsumer` items): the counterpart of `DataProviderScheme`. Like it, it has a fixed id and version in 2.1 (`DATA_CONSUMERS`, `1.0`).
- **`OrganisationUnitScheme`** (`OrganisationUnit` items): an agency's internal units, hierarchical.

## Now

Both are refused on read by name.

sdmx1 and pysdmx model `DataConsumerScheme`. Neither models `OrganisationUnitScheme`.

## Done when

The milestone's checklist holds for both. They reuse the existing organisation-scheme machinery: the fixed-version rule of #416, and organisation scheme maps (#448), which can already name either kind as an end.
