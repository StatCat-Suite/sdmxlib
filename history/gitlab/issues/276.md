# Issue #276: q-3: fourteen tables have no query DSL — 76 hand-written sites, mostly artefact discovery

We ship a query DSL for `sdmx.code` and `sdmx.hierarchy_node`. Consumers query **fourteen other tables** by hand — 76 of the 113 sites.

| table | sites | what they ask it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sdmx.dataflow` | 22 | discovery: which flows exist, which match a category or concept |
| `sdmx.dsd_component` | 21 | "which dimensions does this flow have", joined at catalog scale |
| `sdmx.concept` | 8 | concept-driven discovery |
| `metadata_target` / `_value` / `_set` | 13 | reference metadata attached to an artefact |
| `localized_text`, `artefact`, `annotation`, `hierarchy_association`, … | 12 | labels, spine rows, annotations |

The largest single pattern across these is **concept-driven discovery** — `sdmx.dsd_component ⋈ sdmx.dataflow ⋈ sdmx.concept`, "find every dataflow whose DSD uses concept X". That is the most common question asked of an SDMX catalog and there is no way to express it.

## Why the in-memory API is not the answer

`dsd.dimensions` and `Query[T]` already answer these for a *resolved* artefact. Consumers do not use them because at catalog scale the question is a join: "across 800 dataflows, which use REF_AREA" is not 800 `resolve()` calls. `Query[T]` filters an in-memory collection; these need SQL.

## The decision this issue exists for

**One generic artefact query, or one DSL per table?**

- *Per table* mirrors `code_query` / `hierarchy_query` and gives each the right fields, but it is fourteen more modules, and #267 has just shown how expensive parallel DSLs are to keep from drifting.
- *Generic* — one query over `sdmx.artefact` with per-type extension joins — is one module, but the field surface becomes dynamic and the type safety #267 preserved is hard to keep.

A likely middle: a small shared query over the artefact spine (id, agency, version, urn, name, annotations — the `MaintainableArtefact` surface every type has), plus targeted joins for the two or three genuinely distinct shapes (`dsd_component`, `metadata_target`). Most of the 76 sites are spine questions with a filter.

Do **not** open this until #274 and #275 land. They will have answered whether projection and aggregation live in a record or a tree, and the answer determines whether fourteen tables is a reasonable amount of surface to generate or an unreasonable amount to hand-write.

## Scope when it opens

- Pick the shape above with reasoning recorded.
- Cover the spine questions first — they are the bulk.
- `dsd_component` is the highest-value single addition after the spine; it alone is 21 sites.
- Reference metadata (`metadata_target`) is its own shape and may deserve its own issue.

## Test gates

- Differential properties per artefact kind, against the in-memory `Query[T]` where one exists — an unusually good oracle, since it answers the same question over resolved objects.
- The concept-discovery join covered end to end.
- **Cookbook**: a discovery page — find dataflows by concept, by category, by agency. This is the query prospective users will try first and currently cannot.
