Part of the [Cleanup follow-ups](../-/milestones/4) milestone. Sequel to !48.

Phase 4 deferred three reader functions that have substantive version-specific differences. Bringing them into \`_common.py\` requires either a version flag or a small extension hook — non-trivial design call:

## Targets
- [ ] **\`_read_concept_schemes\`** (62-line diff). The bodies differ enough that a simple superset isn't safe. Look at the diff and pick: per-concept hook, version flag, or split helper.
- [ ] **\`_read_constraints\`** (22-line diff). Closer to identical; check if a small adapter handles both shapes.
- [ ] **\`_read_hierarchical_code\`** (60-line diff — 3.1 adds \`Level\` support, 3.0 has the \`<Code>\` child shape, 2.1 uses \`<CodeID>\` with inline ref attrs). This one's the biggest design call.

## Approach
Likely a two-pronged design:
1. Where the *traversal* is shared but a *node parser* differs, extract the inner parser as a callable parameter on the common reader.
2. Where the *signature* differs (e.g. \`_read_hierarchical_code\` in 3.0 takes \`index\` + \`levels_by_id\`), version-flag at the top.

## Acceptance
- \`just ci\` passes — all SDMX-ML round-trip tests, including the 3.0 hierarchy + level tests, still green.
- Net line reduction across \`sdmx_ml21/reader.py\` + \`sdmx_ml30/reader.py\` + \`sdmx_ml/_common.py\` of at least 100 lines.
- No regressions on FMR cassettes (some FMR outputs use URN-text refs the 3.0 reader handles).

## Out of scope
- 5 of 8 test files with their own \`_make_dsd\` variants. Phase 3 (!47) migrated 3. The remaining ones embed test-specific shapes; revisit only if they get in the way.
