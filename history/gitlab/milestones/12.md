# Milestone %12: Cross-version conversion: convert what converts, refuse only what cannot

| | |
| --- | --- |
| State | closed |
| Opened | 2026-08-07 |
| Due | — |

A document read as one SDMX-ML version should survive a trip through another and back, wherever the formats allow it. Where they do not, the refusal stays — an explicit error is the right answer, and it is already the behaviour. The goal is to shrink the set of refusals to the ones the standard actually forces.

Measured starting point, running 2.1 -> 3.0 -> 2.1 over the checked-in 2.1 corpus: 4 of 6 documents are blocked and 2 survive with losses. Of the four blocks, one is fundamental (the grouping containers) and three are artificial — our model or our writers, not the formats.

SDMX 3.0 mostly *relocated* or *unified* what it dropped rather than deleting it: AttachmentConstraint folded into DataConstraint, MSD targets moved to Metadataflow and MetadataSet. Those conversions are available to us and are simply not implemented.
