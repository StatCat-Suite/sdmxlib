| | |

Audit of all 163 lint/type suppressions and 203 `Any`-bearing lines in `src/`, done after the ruff 0.16 / pyrefly preset=all hardening (!184).

Verdicts: 24 type-ignores paper over a fixable root cause, 43 are justified in substance but written 20-40 times instead of once, 33 are genuinely justified, and 12 hide complexity rather than types.

Tiers: **[S1]** restores real types where a suppression stands in for one; **[S2]** fixes suppression mechanics; **[S3]** collapses repeated per-site comments into single decisions; **[S4]** tracked debt and investigations.
