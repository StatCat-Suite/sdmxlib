## Scope: measurement, not features

This milestone builds **tests**. Everything the tests found that requires changing what the DSL *expresses or means* moved to %14 — including two defects and two semantics decisions that came out of phase 1. The split is deliberate: a test milestone that starts adding operators stops being a measurement of anything.

Where a %14 item blocks work here, it is named as a prerequisite rather than pulled back in.

## Provenance note (2026-08-09)

Phase 2's #257 and #258 came from reviewing an external testing-strategy proposal for the SQL builder. Three of its recommendations were **already delivered** by phase 1 — differential testing against a reference interpreter, per-test in-memory DuckDB, and most of the algebraic laws — and two were **rejected**:

- **`EXPLAIN`-based plan-shape assertions.** Right technique, wrong time. Filing them before anyone has profiled a slow pushdown inverts `docs/design/caching.md`'s own rule: measure first, and name the slow line. `tests/bench` and `test_perf_regressions.py` already exist for when there is something to measure.
- **Normalised SQL-string comparison.** Same failure mode as a golden file — it passes when the semantics are wrong. The differential properties cover semantics; #257 covers structure. Nothing needs a canonical-string assertion in between.

Its `And` / `Or` / `Not` recommendation was first read here as "does not apply", since neither DSL has those combinators. On inspection that is a functional gap rather than a mismatch, and it became %14.

Two of the proposal's technical claims are wrong, both measured against DuckDB rather than argued, and both corrected in #262: `NOT (a = 'x')` and `a <> 'x'` select identical rows, and `Not(Not(x)) ≡ x` does **not** fail on nullable columns — `NOT` is an involution in three-valued logic. The law that fails is excluded middle.
