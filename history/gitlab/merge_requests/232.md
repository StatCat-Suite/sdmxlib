Completes #223 — every field `StructureMessage` carries now has generative, schema-validated round-trip coverage, and the excuse list is empty.

Six findings, all confirmed against the vendored schemas and all fixed. See #226 for detail. Headline: **AttachmentConstraint was dropped silently by 3.0, 3.1 and SDMX-JSON** — #225 in the opposite direction, since SDMX 3.0 removed the artefact outright — and **SDMX-ML 2.1 was emitting two elements that do not exist in 2.1** (`DataStructureMap`, `DataflowMap`), with the 2.1 reader looking for the same invented names, so the pair round-tripped cleanly through sdmxlib while failing XSD validation everywhere else.

Plus three SDMX-JSON map shapes the schema rejects (`conceptMaps`/`categoryMaps` should be `itemMaps` of `SingleValueMappingType`; `ComponentMap` needs `source`/`target` arrays), and an `xs:unique` identity constraint on CubeRegion inclusion that nothing enforced.

One gap stays open and is documented on #226: **2.1 cannot carry a StructureMap at all**, because every component `<Ref>` needs a class/package/containerID that `ComponentMap` does not model. Refused rather than emitted wrongly.

The refusal table is now keyed by wire version rather than being 2.1-only, and its honesty test accepts two justifications — the writer drops the artefact, or emits it in a form the official schema rejects — while still failing on a clean schema-valid round-trip, which would mean refusing a serialisation that works.

**None of the six was reachable from any fixture in the tree.** Same structural blind spot as #225.
