## Summary

- Extends `RepresentationMap` so source/target accept either a codelist `Ref` or a primitive SDMX `DataType` literal — the 3.0 RepresentationMapType has always permitted both, but the model only carried the codelist case, so maps like "codelist → Integer" silently lost their target on read.
- Adds `MapDataType` + `MapEndpoint = AnyRef | MapDataType`; wires the union through SDMX-ML 3.0 and SDMX-JSON 2.1 readers/writers.
- SDMX-ML 2.1 writer and DuckDB storage silently drop the dataType variant (neither schema can carry it).
- Extends `with_representation_map_variants.xml` with an `RM_DATATYPE` entry (codelist → Integer) and adds reader assertions.

Closes #131.
