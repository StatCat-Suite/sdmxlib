## What pointing the survey at the real planner found

The survey test carried its own prototype of the rewrite search — that is how the rewrite got designed. Keeping it once `_pushdown` grew the real one would have been two implementations free to drift, with the test agreeing with itself while the library did something else. Replacing it surfaced everything below.

**A bug the prototype was hiding.** It checked whether the *inner* scan of a rewrite was bounded, never the outer one. With only `dsd.agency` stated, the rewrite lifts the sole predicate into the sub-scan and leaves the outer scan carrying nothing — `GET /dataflow` for every agency, then a local membership. §5's per-scan bound arriving from the direction it was not drafted for. The check now fires on `residual or memberships`, because lifting every residual empties the list and the old condition passed silently.

So **the headline query needs two agency filters, one per scan**, where the earlier write-up said one.

## The number, corrected rather than patched

§10 read 6/7/2/5 — 75 % servable — against the prototype. It is now 8/2/1/12 over 23 shapes, 11 servable. The drop is the rule working: every query with no agency on one of its scans genuinely cannot be answered without fetching a registry.

## Two gaps closed, and a prediction retracted

- `Capability.scans` — which relations a backend can scan at all. REST has no endpoint for "all codes".
- `bounding` keyed by root — one flat set could only ever mean "agency on the root".

`root_key` splits them and the split *is* the resource list; the scannable set is derived from the accessors. **This changed seven refusals from misleading to actionable and did not move the count** — the doc says so, having predicted otherwise. `q.code` previously refused with "add an agency", a fix that could not work.

Serving an item-rooted query needs a third thing: scan the owner and *explode* its items into rows. A new step kind, not a declaration.

## Typing

- `Membership` moved from `Push` to `Residual`. §1 and §6 both draw it as a LOCAL step and they are right — no SDMX REST URL takes a set of urns, and on `Push` it could read as a bounding constraint, which it is not.
- `rebase` is now `[R, C]`: re-rooting changes the *subject*, so returning `Pred[R]` type-checked and lied. One cast carries the phantom, in place of six identical ones at the `case` arms; a second sits at `_relations`, the planner's only dynamic boundary.
