## Changes

- **`usage` is never None.** `Attribute.usage` and `Measure.usage` default to `AttributeUsage.OPTIONAL`, as SDMX 3.x's schema defaults `@usage`. An unstated usage used to be written to 2.1 as Conditional and read back OPTIONAL, with no declaration.
  - The SDMX-ML 3.x and SDMX-JSON writers now always state it.
  - The 2.1 measure-usage gap now fires only for a mandatory measure. That removes BIS 23, OECD 427 and SDMX 20 spurious gap reports.
  - The fidelity guard treats `usage="optional"` as the schema default.
- **`attachment=None` is refused on write**, for an attribute or a DSD's metadata attribute usage, in every format. All of them require the relationship, and every writer used to invent a dataset-level one.
  - **Readers** read a missing `AttributeRelationship` as DATASET. The first sweep found that FMR omits it: IMF has 16 attributes and 3,047 usages without one. Refusing those stopped 166 of IMF's 186 DSDs, so the design was revisited with you.
- **AttachmentGroup** (2.1-only) is left out of 3.x and SDMX-JSON as a declared gap. Before, the message was refused.
