Second of the three agreed MRs after the attribute census (!553): the maintainable flags.

## Model (sdmx1's shape, as decided)
- `is_external_reference: bool = False`, `service_url` and `structure_url` on every maintainable, including `MetadataSet`. They are documented once on `MaintainableArtefact`.
- `is_partial: bool = False` on `Codelist` (and its geographic kinds), `ConceptScheme`, `CategoryScheme`, `AgencyScheme`, `DataProviderScheme` and `MetadataProviderScheme`.

## Formats
- **SDMX-ML 2.1, 3.0, 3.1:** all four are written and read. The writer uses one shared helper, `write_reference_attrs`, now called from the ten 3.x writers that set attributes inline. Readers splat `read_reference_attrs`.
- **3.0 metadata message:** carries the trio on `MetadataSet`. Its reader now also reads `uri`, which the writer always wrote and the reader never read.
- **SDMX-JSON:** carries `isExternalReference` (on `MaintainableJson`) and `isPartial` (on a new `ItemSchemeJson` base). JSON has no `serviceURL` or `structureURL`; that's a declared gap.
- **2.1 maps and hierarchies:** written inside a StructureSet or HierarchicalCodelist, which are the maintainable elements, so these can't carry the trio; declared gap.

## Store — schema 2.33
- `artefact.service_url`, `structure_url` and `is_partial`. The `is_external_ref` column, declared since the first schema and never written, is now written.
- `LazyCodelist` and `LazyHierarchy` carry the fields and pass them on when materialised.
- **Bundle migrated in place:** three `ADD COLUMN`s and a stamp to 2.33, 698 artefacts. DuckDB can't add a column with a NOT NULL constraint, so `is_partial` was added with `DEFAULT FALSE` only. Backup at `meta.duckdb.2.32.bak`.

## Losses and tests
- The `@isPartial` and `@isExternalReference|@serviceURL|@structureURL` rows are deleted, with their injections and census entries.
- `maximal` sets every field off its default. `shapes` sets the trio on the category scheme map and attachment constraint.
- The TWG 2.1 sample now reports 12 fewer JSON losses.
- New round-trip tests cover a metadata set in 3.0 XML and in JSON.
