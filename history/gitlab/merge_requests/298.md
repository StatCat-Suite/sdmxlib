## The audit

Identity constraints were the last kind, and the easiest to miss by eye: an `xs:unique` sits *beside* an element declaration rather than inside its type, so reading `CodelistType` tells you nothing about `UniqueCodelist` hanging off `<Codelists>`.

## The gap

Nothing stopped a message carrying **two artefacts of the same class with the same id, agency and version**. Both serialised happily and every schema rejected the result:

```python
StructureMessage(codelists=[Codelist(id="CL", maintainer="AG", version="1.0", ...),
                            Codelist(id="CL", maintainer="AG", version="1.0", ...)])
# before: serialised fine, XSD rejected it, in 2.1 and 3.0 alike
# now:    ValidationError naming the class, the identity and the count
```

Version-independent — all three state it. Adding the check broke **zero** existing tests.

Right about the size. Wrong about the location. It spot-checked item-id uniqueness, found it covered, and inferred the remaining risk sat in the odd constraints like CubeRegion. ## The mapping is the deliverable

`_STATUS` resolves every constraint to a verdict and names the rule where one exists, so the next schema refresh reports which constraints are new or gone instead of needing a re-audit by hand. Three guards keep it honest:

- unmapped shapes fail, naming them;
- dead entries fail, so a removed constraint cannot leave a stale claim of coverage;
- an extraction returning nothing fails — which it did once, while the extractor was being written, reporting a clean bill over an empty set.

The first of those earned its place immediately: the 2.1-only seventh shape was missed on my first pass and the guard caught it.

## A separate defect found on the way

Chasing that seventh shape turned up **#305**: a DSD containing a `MeasureDimension` raises `AttributeError` from validation and cannot be serialised in *any* version. `MeasureDimension` deliberately is not a `Component` subclass and carries no `enumeration_format`; validation and all four writers assume otherwise.

I reverted the one-line fix I had made for it — the writers are affected too, so it needs its own change and its own tests rather than riding along in an audit. `SingleMeasureDimension` is therefore recorded as *unreachable*, not enforced: there is nothing to enforce it against until #305 is fixed.
