Step 6**b** of `docs/design/registry-and-catalog.md` §6, against the registry
6a (!365) made capable. `catalog.py` is deleted; nothing replaces it.

## The migration was mechanical, and that is the finding

**Every** `from_parts(structural=X, bindings=X, data=…)` in the suite passed
the *same registry* for all three roles. That is the clearest evidence the
composition had nothing to compose — so the rewrite is "drop the wrapper, keep
`X`", and the shared test factory collapsed to `return local`.

**30 of the catalog's own tests then passed unchanged** against the registry,
which is why that file was repointed and renamed (`test_registry_lifecycle.py`)
rather than deleted. Only four assertions were catalog-specific: a `repr`
naming the composed class, two refusal messages, and an export check.

## Consumers

`builders/contentconstraint.py` and `builders/metadata_annotation.py` took a
catalog for exactly three things — `binding_for`, `structural.get`, and
`data.connection` — and a registry has all three, so they now take one.

`DataflowEntry` moves to `model/binding.py`, beside the binding it wraps.

## One test died rather than migrating

`browse()` refusing a bindings registry that lacks `get_all`. That failure mode
existed *only* because the three roles could be different objects — a registry
browses its own bindings and `get_all` is on the class. Restoring the test would
mean restoring the composition, so a comment records it in place rather than
leaving a silent gap.

## What is deliberately not here

The **`LocalRegistry` / `store=` renames.** They are pure renames over a surface
this step is already reshaping, and folding them in would produce one diff where
a reviewer cannot tell a rename from a behaviour change. They want their own
step.

Docs rewritten: `api.md`, `federation.md` (its end-to-end example now shows
federated *structure* into a local registry that owns the lake),
`reference_metadata.md`, `rest_facade.md`.

Gate: `just check`, 5408 unit, 176 docs, 52 integration green; `Any` at
baseline 48.
