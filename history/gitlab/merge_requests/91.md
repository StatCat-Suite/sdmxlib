## Summary

Single-point fix: \`sys.intern()\` lang keys inside \`InternationalString.__init__\` and \`.of()\`. Lang codes are a tiny finite set (\`en\`, \`fr\`, \`de\`, BCP-47 variants) repeated thousands of times across every read of a real SDMX payload. Interning collapses them to shared \`str\` objects.

Interning at the model layer covers every entry point — XML readers, JSON readers, attrs converter, user construction — without touching the 5+ reader sites individually.
