Recommendations 1 and 2 from the %17 review.

**`filterwarnings = ["error"]`, with zero ignore entries.** A warning
that cannot fail is a warning nobody reads — the same argument CLAUDE.md
makes for ruff, where two `# noqa`-in-a-doc-comment warnings survived
three milestones by being routine noise. The cost was measured first:
the whole suite raised exactly two distinct warnings.

Neither is now suppressed. Starlette's was telling us to install
`httpx2`, so we did (test-only, like fastapi; sdmxlib's client stays on
httpx). The other was a stub artefact, not library behaviour — the
federation page opens an ECB registry and an ESTAT one over one
`cache.duckdb`, and `serving` answers both with the *same* fixture, so
the store legitimately warns that a spine row is being overwritten by a
second source. Real providers publish disjoint agencies. Scoped to that
one setup and matched on the message, so any other warning a doc page
raises still fails.

**`formats/sdmx_json/_data_common.py`: 10 `Any` → 0**, and the ratchet
goes 48 → 38. Every one was a decoded observation *cell* —
`list[list[Any]]`, `dict[str, list[Any]]` — held, appended, and handed to
`pl.LazyFrame`. Nothing calls a method on one, which is the litmus for
`object` over `Any`.

`object` then refused `int(raw)` on an attribute slot, and that refusal
was worth having: a payload whose slot is an array raised a bare
`TypeError` out of `sdmxlib.formats.parse` — uncatchable as a parse
failure, the exact thing `formats/_coerce` exists to prevent (#247). Now
`_wire_position` narrows and raises `SdmxParseError`, which subclasses
`ValueError` so the readers' documented contract is unchanged. Three
tests cover it, and reverting the narrowing fails two of them.

The remaining 38 are the categories CLAUDE.md sanctions: `SdmxUrn[Any]`
phantoms that recover the parameter, wire-boundary maps, and two
documented `-> Any` returns.
