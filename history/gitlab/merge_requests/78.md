## Summary

- New `AttachmentConstraint` maintainable in `model/constraint.py` alongside `DataConstraint`. Sibling, not subclass — different XSD type, narrower attachment shape, no CubeRegion/ReleaseCalendar.
- New `DataKey` value type: a tuple of `(dim_id, value)` pairs (single value per dimension), modelling `<com:DataKeyType>`.
- `StructureMessage.attachment_constraints` slot (`ArtefactList[AttachmentConstraint]`); included in `all_artefacts()`.
- SDMX-ML 2.1 reader parses `<str:AttachmentConstraint>` siblings of `<str:ContentConstraint>` inside `<str:Constraints>`, including `<str:DataKeySet>/<str:Key>/<com:KeyValue>/<com:Value>`. The `Key` element is in `str:` namespace (locally declared in `DataKeySetType` in the structure XSD); `KeyValue`/`Value` are in `com:` (declared in `common:DataKeyType`).
- SDMX-ML 2.1 writer emits them after ContentConstraints in the same `Constraints` container, with required `isIncluded="true"` per `KeySetType`.
- 3.0 and 3.1 writers silently drop the slot — no XSD equivalent in those versions.
- `minimal.xml` 2.1 fixture extended with one AttachmentConstraint; XSD-valid.
