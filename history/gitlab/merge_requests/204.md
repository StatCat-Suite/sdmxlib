## What changed

`make_writer_helpers` was a **335-line function holding 22 nested `def`s**, returning a frozen dataclass of closures. It is now a class: namespaces and pre-built tags in `__init__`, the closures as methods.

## Both suppressions dissolved

`# noqa: C901, PLR0915` is gone from it — **not** by writing anything to satisfy the linter. Ruff sums nested `def` complexity into the enclosing function, so 22 individually-simple closures scored as one (76 complexity, 221 statements). As methods each is scored separately and all pass.

Complexity codes in `src/`: **12** at #199 → **9** after !201 → **7** now.

Closure-cell vs instance-attribute access, measured over 2M iterations: **12.5 ns** vs **12.4 ns** per call.

## Three transform errors, all caught before landing

Worth recording, because a 300-line mechanical transformation is exactly where silent breakage hides:

1. **Text regex corrupted a docstring** — `"""Append com:Name…"""` became `"""Append self.com:Name…"""` — and mangled a multi-line signature. Redone with **position-based AST edits**, which cannot touch string contents.
2. `__init__` assigned the pre-built tags as *locals*: the rewrite qualified `Load` contexts but not assignment targets. 10 `reportAttributeAccessIssue` errors from basedpyright.
3. The old dataclass exposed the private `_write_scheme_map` under the **public** name `write_scheme_map`. The conversion kept it private, breaking three call sites in the 2.1 writer.

## Not in this MR

The **reader** factory keeps its suppressions — larger (670 lines, 24 defs) and carrying generic Protocol members (`resolve_ref[T]`, `resolve_ref_text[T]`, `read_representation[T]`) that need care rather than the same script. And the prefixed-path conversion for `_c`/`_s`/`_m` is separate again. Both remain in #209.

## Comments

### 2026-08-05

**Correcting the framing in this MR's description.** It says the C901/PLR0915 scores were "largely an artifact of ruff summing nested `def`s", and draws a parallel to SLF001 — a rule that flags correct code it cannot structurally understand. That parallel is wrong, and it lets a bad design off too lightly.

C901 was flagging genuine convolutedness. A closure factory that takes three namespace parameters, builds ten strings as locals, defines 22 functions closing over all of it, and packs them into a frozen dataclass of `Callable` fields **is** more complex than a class with 22 methods. The score of 76 was earned, not an artifact.

The costs were concrete and visible in the code:

* **Signatures erased.** Three writer fields were typed `Callable[..., None]` — arity, parameter names and types all gone. As methods they carry their real signatures, with nothing written to achieve it.
* **Three Protocol classes exist only to work around the pattern.** `_ResolveRef`, `_ResolveRefText`, `_ReadRepresentation` say so in their own docstrings: *"A plain `Callable` annotation cannot carry a free type parameter"*. A method is generic for free. I added one of these myself in !189 without registering what it was telling me.
* **Every helper declared twice** — once as a nested `def`, once as a dataclass field whose hand-maintained type can drift from it.

So the honest summary of this change is: **the linter was right, the design was worse, and this replaces it.** Not "the metric was measuring the wrong thing and the suppressions fall out as a side effect."

Everything factual in the description stands — the measurements, the byte-identical output, the three transform errors. Only the interpretation was self-serving.
