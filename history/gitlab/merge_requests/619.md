## What pysdmx 1.20.0 does with our 3.1

**It refuses the document** on 19 fields its model lacks, and on 2 it models:
- a group's annotations;
- a related `<Dimension optional>`, which it reads as 2.1's `<Ref>` and fails with KeyError 'Ref'.

Its writer also refuses a nameless ValueItem. ValueItemType makes the name optional, and FMR's reader crashes on the same item.

**It changes meaning:**
- a key value a region excludes comes back included;
- minOccurs/maxOccurs fall to the defaults;
- a mandatory measure becomes optional (the writer omits `usage`);
- an attribute related to a dimension and a measure moves to the observation, and an observation-level one gains a measure relationship;
- `isMultiLingual="true"` is read as false;
- a date end bound becomes the day's first instant;
- an attribute with no representation gets an empty TextFormat, overriding its concept's.

**It writes invalid or corrupt values:**
- localised AnnotationURLs come out as a Python list repr, which fails the XSD;
- a multi-valued FixedValueMap and a regex or substring SourceValue come out as Python reprs; these are still schema-valid strings, so the corruption is silent;
- the ValueList is written inside `str:Codelists`;
- a constraint's CubeRegion comes before its DataKeySets.

**It skips classes silently:** scheme maps and geographic codelists vanish without an error.

**It drops a lot,** by model gap, reader gap or writer gap; each rule says which. Examples: links, non-English text, sentinels, concept roles, code and concept parents, `serviceURL`/`structureURL` (read, then not written), and uri and validity in several places it does model.

Nothing here is ours: what was sent validates against the 3.1 XSD, and every difference traces to pysdmx's model, reader or writer. One observation: our reader accepted pysdmx's out-of-order DataConstraint without comment, since it reads by element, not sequence.
