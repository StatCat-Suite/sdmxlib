## Why pyrefly, not basedpyright

basedpyright (`standard`) has **no narrow rule** for empty-container→Any. Its only
option, `reportUnknownVariableType`, floods with ~159 third-party (duckdb /
pyarrow) `Unknown`s — measured directly — which is exactly why `standard` mode is
used and that rule is off (per CLAUDE.md). This class of finding is the gap
`pyrefly.toml` exists to cover.
