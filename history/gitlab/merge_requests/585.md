Closes #448.

SDMX-ML 2.1 (inside a `StructureSet`), 3.0, 3.1 and SDMX-JSON all carry an `OrganisationSchemeMap`. The model had no class for it, so the 3.x and JSON readers refused it and 2.1 reported it lost.

## What changes

- **Model:** `sl.OrganisationSchemeMap`, shaped like `ConceptSchemeMap`: `source` and `target` refs, and `maps` of `ItemMap` pairs. It lives in the new `StructureMessage.organisation_scheme_maps`. Its ends may be schemes of different kinds, for example agencies onto data providers.
- **Formats:**
  - 2.1 reads and writes it inside a `StructureSet`, with the `<map>@<set>` id rule;
  - 3.x as `OrganisationSchemeMaps`;
  - SDMX-JSON as `organisationSchemeMaps`.
  - The 2.1 `LOSSES` row and the 3.x/JSON "unmodelled" entries are removed.
- **Store:** a new `organisation_scheme_map` table, schema **2.46**. Its pairs share `item_map` with the other scheme maps. The storage readers for the three scheme maps now share one generic `_read_item_scheme_map`.
- **Query:** `sl.q.organisation_scheme_map`. As you decided, `source` and `target` hop to the generic maintainable, since an end can be any organisation scheme kind, including unmodelled ones. Example: `q.organisation_scheme_map.source.id == "AGENCIES"`. They are declared in `_POLYMORPHIC` with that reason.
- **Tests:**
  - The maximal fixture gains one, inside the SS set, and a Hypothesis strategy covers it in the round-trip properties.
  - `test_organisation_scheme_map.py` checks:
    - every format reads back what it wrote, schema-valid;
    - the 2.1 `@set` naming;
    - the store, and both query hops.
  - Coverage guards are updated: census, read losses, field coverage, XSD mapping, ref-field count, collect roots.
