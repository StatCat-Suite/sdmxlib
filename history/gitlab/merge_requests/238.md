**Closes half of #225 and corrects the other half.**

The issue recorded both missing 2.1 emitters as *"unimplemented, not unrepresentable — lift by writing the emitters"*. That turned out to be true of one of them.

## Metadataflow — implemented

2.1's `MetadataflowType` is a plain StructureUsage: `Annotations?, Name, Description?, Structure?`. The writer mirrors the existing Dataflow one almost exactly, and so does the reader. Identity, name, description, annotations, validity dates and the MSD reference all round-trip.

What 2.1 **cannot** carry is `targets` — it has no `<Target>` element at all, where SDMX 3.0 added one and made it mandatory. Recorded in `KNOWN_GAPS` rather than refused (refusing would block every 2.1 downgrade rather than an unusual one), and asserted in **both** directions: 2.1 dropping them and 3.0 keeping them are each a test failure if they change.

## MetadataStructure — not an emitter, and stays refused

2.1's `MetadataStructureComponents` is `minOccurs="1"`, and its type requires **both** a `<MetadataTarget>` and a `<ReportStructure>` — with a MetadataTarget in turn requiring one of five target-kind children.

That is the reporting layer SDMX 3.0 deleted, and this model follows 3.0: a flat tree of `MetadataAttribute` and nothing saying what the metadata is reported *against* in the 2.1 sense. Writing one would mean inventing a target kind and a report structure, then discarding them on read — a 3.0 → 2.1 → 3.0 round-trip would not return what it was given.

So the refusal stays, with its reason changed from "the writer does not implement it" to what the schema actually requires. Same call as 2.1 component maps needing a `class`/`package` the model does not carry (#226): **refuse rather than guess.**

## Controls

Four mutations verified to fail: drop the writer section, drop the reader, drop the `Structure` reference, rename the `KNOWN_GAPS` entry.
