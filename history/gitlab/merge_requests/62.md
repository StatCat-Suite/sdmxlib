## Conformance bugs fixed

### 1. HierarchicalCode emitted \`<Name>\` and \`<Description>\`
Per \`HierarchicalCodeType\` (extension of \`HierarchicalCodeBaseType\`, a restriction of \`IdentifiableType\`), only Annotations + the Code|CodeID choice + nested HierarchicalCode children are allowed on a HierarchicalCode element. Name/Description live on the referenced Codelist item, not on the hierarchy node. Both dropped from the writer; the reader stops looking for them.

### 2. \`<CodeID>\` used without its required \`<CodelistAliasRef>\` partner
\`HierarchicalCodeType\` has a choice between:
- \`<Code>\` — full Ref via \`CodeReferenceType\`
- \`<CodelistAliasRef> + <CodeID>\` — alias-form sequence requiring a matching \`<IncludedCodelist alias=...>\` on the containing HCL

Our writer emitted \`<CodeID>\` alone, which is invalid in either form. Switched to the \`<Code>\` form because our model carries a fully-resolved code Ref and has no alias table to populate. The reader now accepts both forms — \`<Code>\` for new output, legacy \`<CodeID>\` for anything written by the old writer — and prefers \`maintainableParentVersion\` over the legacy \`version\` attribute when reading the item Ref.
