Part 1 of #457: the FMR oracle for SDMX-ML 2.1 and 3.0. Part 2 (as agreed) adds pysdmx as the reader-oracle for SDMX-ML 3.1, which FMR 12.4.2 cannot read at all. Leaves #457 open.

## What it is
- `bench/fmr/` — `just fmr-record` sends the maximal fixture and the version shapes to a **local** FMR (`sdmxio/fmr-mysql:12.4.2` under Colima; setup in `bench/fmr/README.md`), and records what it accepts and what it serves back in SDMX-ML 2.1/3.0 and SDMX-JSON 1.0/2.0. Never in `ci`, never against a public registry.
- `tests/integration/fmr/12.4.2/` — the recordings (~740 KB).
- `tests/integration/test_fmr_oracle.py` — 22 tests replaying them offline in `just ci`.

## The guards
- **What was sent is what we send now** — byte-equal to the fixtures + ledger today, so a fixture change forces a re-record.
- **Adjustments** (`bench/fmr/ledger.py`, 39): what FMR refuses in our documents, each classed `fmr-deviation` (XSD declares it) / `fmr-bug` / `fmr-gap` / `fmr-rule` / `registry-rule` (IM-backed), each *needed* — the recording shows FMR refusing the document without it, or accepting fewer artefacts (FMR's silent truncation).
- **Differences** (`tests/support/fmr_differences.py`, 35 rules): every difference between what we sent and what FMR served, read through our reader beyond `formats.gaps`, classified by **exactly one** rule, every rule used. Disjointness was added after the sabotage test showed a broad rule absorbing a narrow one.
- **FMR's invalid output** is exactly the declared 14 of 23 responses (XSD / vendored JSON schema), and every `fmr-invalid` classification rests on one of them.
- **FMR still reads and serves no SDMX-ML 3.1** (400 on submit, 406 on fetch).

## Found — ours
- #489 SDMX-JSON 1.0 `attributeRelationship: {"none": {}}` (schema-valid) refuses the DSD.

- The shape fixture mapped to codes its codelists lack, at a wrong arity — fixed here; the missing checks noted on #483.

## Found — FMR 12.4.2
- **Silent truncation**: a ValueItem with no Name crashes its 3.0 reader; it persisted 7 of 28 artefacts and reported success.
- **Changed meaning**: a month `endTime` `2030-12` becomes `2030-12-01T23:59:59`; `maxOccurs="unbounded"` dropped; a metadata attribute's 0..1 becomes 1..unbounded; lower-case ids upper-cased.
- **Lost**: a 2.1 StructureSet's concept/organisation/category scheme maps, node levels, time-range selections, links, all but one AnnotationURL, descriptor annotations.
- **Invalid output**: annotation links with no `rel` (JSON), annotation children out of order (3.0), `allowed` for `Allowed`, Enumerations naming concept schemes / value lists.
- **Deviations from the XSD** it refuses on input: SentinelValue, ConceptRole, com:Link on components, QueryableDataSource, geo codes in a codelist, MetadataAttributeUsage, EpochMap, and more — each with its XSD line in the ledger.
