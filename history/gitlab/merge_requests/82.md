## Summary

ItemList already maintains a \`_by_id\` dict so lookup itself is O(1); the cost surfaced in MR !81's Phase 3 bench (\`test_nav_dim_lookup\` = 70 ns vs direct dict at 31 ns) was the \`__getitem__\` dispatch: two \`isinstance\` checks (int, slice) before the dict hit.

Reorder so \`isinstance(index, str)\` runs first — the common case (\`dsd.dimensions[\"FREQ\"]\`, \`cl.codes[\"A\"]\`) short-circuits before paying for the other two checks.
