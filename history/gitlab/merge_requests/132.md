## What changed

## Design note

Baking `series_attributes_join_keys` onto the binding (caching-doc tier 3) was **deferred**: it needs a build-time connection + materialized tables, new storage round-trip (overlaps #160), a binding migration, and carries a staleness hazard, for negligible gain over the cache. This change is a strict subset, so baking stays a clean follow-up.
