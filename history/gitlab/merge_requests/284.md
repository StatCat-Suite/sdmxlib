Closes #273. A decision record; the deliverable is the decision, committed to `docs/design/query-composition.md` rather than left in an issue comment.

## Decided: no separate IR for the structural DSLs

#273 set its own test — attempt #270's canonical form without a new IR — and it fits. Predicates are frozen attrs: hashable and sortable by a derived key `(type name, fields)`, though not by `<`, so it needs an explicit key function. Nothing but emitted clause order reads predicate order, so canonicalizing at construction costs no fidelity anything consumes. The complication the issue anticipated — order-sensitive sort keys — dissolved when #294 made `order_by` replace.

## The coverage evidence had gone stale

The issue rests on a survey of six consuming applications, taken before #275 (projection + aggregation), #276 (artefact spine) and #281 (reported metadata). Re-measured by attempting each named query shape: **13 of 18 expressible**. "Find dataflows using concept X" — called the most common discovery query and credited with most of the `dsd_component`/`dataflow`/`concept` sites — is now `artefact.uses_concept(...)`. "2 of 16 tables, ~33% of sites" understates current coverage substantially.

The five remaining gaps are not rewrites an IR would enable — expression-valued projection, array aggregation, joins over caller tables, PIVOT, window functions — each a feature decision of its own. The array-aggregation one is a *refusal*: annotations reach the row by correlated EXISTS (#254), and aggregating them means the join EXISTS exists to avoid.

## The data plane gets a different answer

`_build_query` does interpolate caller `where` / `columns` / `order_by` as SQL text. But it must **not** share a representation with the structural DSLs, whose safety property is that they never accept SQL text at all — merging would give the safe surface a hole it does not have. Filed as #297, framed as an injection-surface problem.

Normalization gets no home until a second rewrite exists.
