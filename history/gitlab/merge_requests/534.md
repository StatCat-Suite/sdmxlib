Full registry sweep re-run over everything merged into the collecting branch since the last baseline. Includes one census fix the run turned up.

## Census fix

`read_lost` measures the reader by writing back what was read in the source's own format. An artefact the write check refuses was never written back, so every path it held counted as lost. Every codelist that SDMX-ML 3.0 now refuses for its parent ids (#432) showed up as read loss: 9 codelists in SDMX, 3 in IMF. Refused artefacts are now written unchecked for the census only. Two tests in `tests/unit/test_sweep.py` cover this, and both fail without the fix.

Documents with a refusal in their own format were re-run with the fix and spliced into the full summaries: BIS categorisation and codelist; SDMX, OECD and IMF codelist; IMF metadataflow.
