Refs #516. This is the typing decision recorded there.

An observation query's results are now **typed from the DSD**, as a `Dataset`'s are. That covers `frame()` and `select(...).collect()`; columns that are projected under an alias are typed under the alias.

**What gets typed:** only components whose type the DSD declares.
- Codes become their codelist's `pl.Enum`.
- Values become what their `Facet` declares.
- The time dimension gets its declared format.
- A component with no representation **keeps its stored type**. The first version cast these to `schema()`'s `String` fallback and turned stored `DOUBLE`s into text; the suite caught that before it shipped.

**What is untouched:** aggregates and structural queries.

**A value that doesn't fit** raises `ValueError`, as a `Dataset` read does. The message names `.stream()` for the raw rows and `validation_report` for the bad values.

**Fixture fix:** the new rule found a fixture bug. The data cookbook's lake held `REF_AREA = "ON"`, which the KEI codelist doesn't list; it is now `CAN`.

**Tests:** `test_observation_types.py`. Five tests fail on the old code; two discrimination checks pass on both.

**Checks:** no new suppression. `just ci` and `just test-properties` (20×) are green.
