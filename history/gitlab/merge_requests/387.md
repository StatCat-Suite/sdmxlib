## Three findings about the library, not the docs

Each now has a test stating it, rather than a reader discovering it:

- **`resolve=` is `RestRegistry`-only.** `LocalRegistry.get` resolves refs unconditionally — depth is a choice about *requests*, and a local store has already paid for them. Copying the front page's call onto a local registry raises `TypeError`. Noted in `upgrading.md`.
- **`slpl.label` puts the language in the column name** — `FREQ_label_en`, not `FREQ_label` — which is what lets `lang=["en", "fr"]` add both without colliding.
- **`slpl.schema` maps a coded dimension to `pl.Enum`** with its categories, not `pl.String`.
