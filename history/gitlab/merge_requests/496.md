#406's `expect=` overloads take `SdmxUrn[object] | Ref[Any]`, and I justified
the `Any` half in three places with "`Ref` is invariant by design (#289), so
`Ref[object]` would refuse the `Ref[Codelist]` a caller holds".

That is backwards. `Ref` carries an **explicitly covariant** TypeVar for exactly
this assignment. Its own class comment says so and says why the mutable
`_resolved` field does not make covariance unsafe: the field is written once,
package-internally, by `_interning.resolve_ref`, and only with the artefact its
URN already denotes. Both checkers agree — verified before the change and after,
not recalled.

So `Ref[object]` accepts every typed `Ref` a caller has, and the eight `Any` in
those signatures were never doing anything. They are gone; the ratchet is back
to 40 from 48.

## What #289 and #399 do establish

The *other* direction. `Ref[object]` does not go where `Ref[Codelist]` is
wanted, because covariance is one-way — and that is exactly what narrowing
`SdmxUrn.parse` to `SdmxUrn[object]` would require at 52 basedpyright + 27
pyrefly sites. **That measurement stands**; only my explanation of it was
inverted. Both docstrings now state the direction rather than the label.

## Where the wrong claim was written down

Four places, all mine, all corrected:

* `LocalRegistry.get` — the #399 caveat's "two things not to do" list.
* `SdmxUrn.parse` — the note explaining why its phantom stays gradual.
* `tools/any_baseline.txt` — the entry justifying 40 → 48, now the entry
  justifying 48 → 40 and saying what it got wrong.
* `test_get_target_contract.IMPL_TARGET_ANNOTATION` — the worst of the four,
  because a wrong spelling written into a *guard* becomes the rule the next
  reader follows.

## The guard that would have caught it

`probe_registry_expect.py` asserted `expect=` against a `Ref[object]` argument
only, which passes under either variance. It now also asserts a typed
`Ref[Codelist]`, which is the discrimination check the file was missing: with
`Ref[Any]` both lines pass, with `Ref[object]` both lines pass, and with an
*invariant* `Ref` the second fails. That is the shape a variance claim needs —
an assignment that only works if the claim is true.

No behaviour changed; `expect=` answers exactly what it answered in 0.62.2.
