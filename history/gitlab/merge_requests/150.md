## The two real findings

**1. Optional passed to `list.index`** (`_data_common.py`)
`observe_batch_time_freq` called `dim_cols.index(self._time_dim_id)` with `self._time_dim_id: str | None`. The `not in dim_cols` / `tracks_freq()` guard makes it non-None at runtime, but neither narrows the attribute for the checker. pyrefly correctly flagged the `str | None` arg; **basedpyright's `reportArgumentType` (on) missed it** — a flow-precision gap, not a disabled rule. Fixed by binding to locals with an explicit `is None` guard so the invariant is visible and both checkers verify it.

**2. Inconsistent `maintainer` override** (`base.py`)
The `MaintainableArtefact` mixin declared `maintainer: Agency | str` while all 20 concrete subclasses declare `maintainer: Agency` (str absorbed by `converter=_to_agency`). Narrowing a *mutable* attribute in subclasses is an LSP inconsistency (pyrefly `bad-override-mutable-attribute` ×20). Reads are always `Agency` post-construction, so the mixin now declares it strictly as `Agency` — aligning base + children and removing the dead `isinstance(m, str)` branch in `agency_id`.

Neither is catchable by a basedpyright config toggle — both are flow/variance precision gaps, exactly pyrefly's complementary niche.
