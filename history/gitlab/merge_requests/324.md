`get()` returns a lazy proxy for a Codelist and a Hierarchy, and
`materialize()` rebuilt the artefact from a hand-listed subset of the
fields that proxy holds. So the two read paths disagreed: `lazy.uri`
returned the stored value, `lazy.materialize().uri` returned `None`.
Materialising for an unrelated reason silently changed the answer.

Eight fields across the two types — uri, valid_from, valid_to,
has_formal_levels, and annotations on both. Annotations were the one
field the proxies genuinely did not have, and are now a property
loading through `annotations_for_urns`; the rest were already in
`__slots__` and simply not passed on.

`Hierarchy.has_formal_levels` was filed under #323 as a storage gap and
was not one: the column held it and the proxy read it. `Hierarchy.uri`
was likewise filed under #320. Filing by symptom put both under the
wrong issue, and both KNOWN_LOSSES entries are corrected here.

The new guard is derived from `attrs.fields()` and builds its own
artefact rather than using `make_codelist()`. That second part is the
part that matters: written against the fixtures, it passed with all
four fields deleted from the fix, because a fixture that never sets
`uri` agrees on `None` whatever `materialize()` does. Re-checked by
deleting each field in turn — all five now fail.
