## What changes
- **Facet bounds:** `Facet.decimals`, `max_length` and `min_length` must be absent or at least 1 (`_rules.positive_integer`). Every SDMX-ML schema (2.1, 3.0, 3.1) types them `xs:positiveInteger`, and every SDMX-JSON schema (1.0, 2.0, 2.1) an integer with minimum 1, so 0 has no form in any format (#224). The check sits on `Facet` itself, so it now covers every representation (measures, concepts, levels, MSD attributes); `validate()` only walked a DSD's dimensions and attributes.
- **Time ranges:** a `TimeRange` needs a start, an end, or both. A field validator on `end` (attrs runs it once every field is set), not `__attrs_post_init__`, so `tests/support/unchecked.py` can still build one.
- `_check_facet_bounds` and `_check_time_range` leave `validate()`; the IM census drops them (checks 72, non-IM 56).
- **Readers:** an artefact holding either is refused alone, as in part 1. `test_read_refuses_invalid` covers a zero `maxLength` through the 2.1 and 3.0 readers, beside its valid twin.
- **Tests:**
  - the old validate() tests now assert refusal at construction and on `attrs.evolve`;
  - the wire-conformance case for an unbounded range is gone, since the model cannot hold it;
  - the reflection test builds a bounded `TimeRange`.

  Every new or rewritten test failed with the validators removed.
- **Docs:** `docs/upgrading.md`'s #492 section and the CLAUDE.md `_rules` row.
