Part of #421 (MSD row, second half).

SDMX 3.x requires a metadataflow to name at least one target. SDMX-ML 2.1 declares them in the metadata structure instead, so a flow read from 2.1 has none, and every 3.x and SDMX-JSON write refused it.

## What changes

- The SDMX-ML 3.0/3.1 and SDMX-JSON writers derive the targets from the MSD's `IdentifiableObjectTarget`s, one wildcard URN per class. For example, `Dataflow` gives `urn:sdmx:org.sdmx.infomodel.datastructure.Dataflow=*:*(*)`, and an item class gives `…codelist.Code=*:*(*).*`. 2.1 spellings are folded to their 3.x names (`Attribute` → `DataAttribute`).
- The MSD comes from the resolved reference, or else from the message.
- The derivation is declared in `gaps.py`, so `sl.unrepresentable(mdf, "3.0")` reports it.
- Still refused, with the reason named:
  - the MSD is neither resolved nor in the message;
  - the MSD has no `IdentifiableObjectTarget`;
  - a target type no 3.x URN names (`Any`, `ContentConstraint`).
- New API: `Metadataflow.with_derived_targets(msds)` and `.target_misfits(msds)`.
- **Bug fix:** the SDMX-JSON DTO had no `targets` field for a metadataflow, so targets were lost silently on every JSON write. They are now written and read, and the stale `KNOWN_LOSSES` entry is gone.

## Sweep

No live flow is unblocked. BIS's 4 flows reference MSDs its 2.1 endpoint does not serve, and IMF's 1 is a 3.0 flow served without targets. The refusal now says why. OECD and SDMX are unchanged.
