`SchemaInspector` ran `DESCRIBE` and the sidecar uniqueness count on the store's base connection. Concurrent `execute()` on one DuckDB handle overwrite each other's rows (#75). Since `obs.col` started reading physical column types, the inspector runs on every concurrent `obs` query. Under the full parallel suite, `test_alternating_questions_on_one_dataflow_do_not_interleave` failed every run with an `IndexError` in `_described`: a `DESCRIBE` row had come back from another thread's result.

- Both queries now go through `cursor_for`, the thread's own handle.
- The counting spies in `test_data_store_inspect.py` and `test_sidecar_uniqueness.py` now replace `cursor_for` in `sdmxlib.local.sql`, not `_conn`. They count what runs on the real per-thread handle, and two `# type: ignore[assignment]` lines go.
