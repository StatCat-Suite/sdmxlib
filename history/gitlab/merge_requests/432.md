## The dangling column

`metadata_provision_agreement.metadata_provider_urn` was `TEXT NOT NULL`, populated on every write, and resolvable by nothing — a foreign key naming a relation that did not exist. SDMX 3.0 has `MetadataProvider` and `MetadataProviderScheme`; this library modelled neither, while the **data** twin was complete. That asymmetry is why the gap read as an accessor someone had forgotten rather than a missing relation.
