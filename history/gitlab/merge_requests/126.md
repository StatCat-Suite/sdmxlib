## Summary

The durable guard #159 recommended: a generic, data-driven round-trip
property test. It builds a **fully-populated** artefact, round-trips it
through each serializer (SDMX-JSON, SDMX-ML 2.1/3.0, DuckDB storage), and
asserts every wire-representable field survives.

Known **Table B** symmetric gaps (fields neither side of a pair handles)
are marked `xfail(strict=True)`. That does double duty:

- documents the exact gap with a per-entry reason, and
- flips to a **failure** the moment a gap is closed (strict XPASS), so
  the marker gets removed as B work lands.

New, unintended drops (regressions of the merged #157/#158/A fixes)
surface as ordinary failures.

## What it caught

Writing the guard corrected several assumptions from the manual audit and
surfaced gaps the audit missed:

- **Corrected:** `isMultiLingual` on a concept facet, and per-mapping /
  RepresentationMap validity, actually *do* round-trip through JSON and
  SDMX-ML 3.0 — they were wrongly listed as gaps.
- **New gaps found:** SDMX-ML 2.1 drops `concept_role`; SDMX-ML 2.1 omits
  `<str:Contact>` entirely (phones/faxes/x400s lost); SDMX-ML 3.0 drops
  Level `codingFormat`.

These are folded into the xfail set and noted on #159.

## Result

`68 passed, 19 xfailed` — every field that should round-trip does
(regression guard for the merged A fixes), and all 19 open B gaps are
documented and self-tracking.
