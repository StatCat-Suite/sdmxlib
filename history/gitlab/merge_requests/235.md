**Phase 2 of milestone 11 — closes #228.** Inverts the two constraint kinds that state a *shape*, the way earlier work inverted the three that state a value.

## Two functions, not one

`required_children` answers *"must this child always appear"*. `required_choices` answers *"must one of these appear"*. Neither implies the other: a required `xs:choice` obliges a document to carry one of its branches while no single branch is itself required, so an extraction asking only the first question reports nothing at all for it.

The cross-check proves the split is load-bearing. Against the conformance gate's `KNOWN_INVALID` — 22 SDMX-ML failures gathered by *writing documents and validating them*, so ground truth independent of any schema reading — the two extractions split **16 to 6**. Without `required_choices`, a quarter of the observed failures would be invisible.

That cross-check is the extractor's real verification, and it runs in the right direction: from the schema alone, with nothing generated, it predicts failures that were found empirically.

## The nesting walk is the difficulty

An element is strictly required only when its own `minOccurs` is not 0, **no ancestor particle is an `xs:choice`**, and no ancestor group carries `minOccurs="0"`. `complexContent/extension` is followed into the base; `complexContent/restriction` is not, since a restriction redefines the content model rather than adding to it.

A prototype that skipped the choice/group walk reported `CategorisationType` as requiring `Source` and `Target`. It requires neither — a categorisation carrying neither serialises to valid 3.0, verified by writing one. **Over-reporting is the dangerous direction**: acting on it means refusing documents that are fine, which is the mistake !231 already made once with a grep-built table.

So the extraction is anchored two ways:

- **six types pinned to a hand-read** of the schema, each exercising a different part of the walk;
- **five mutations of the walk**, each verified to fail the suite.

The choice-branch mutation initially did **not** fail — all three original hand-checked types passed with choice-awareness deleted, so nothing was guarding the exact over-report the walk exists to prevent. `ConstraintAttachmentType` and `GenericMetadataStructureType` were added to close it.

## Mapped onto the model

`test_every_required_child_maps_to_a_model_field` is the form phase 3 (#229) needs: a required child with **no model field** is a modelling gap that no write-boundary rule can fix, because there would be nothing to require. Knowing which of the two a failure is — before writing the fix — is the point of extracting the schema rather than reading errors.

Writing it caught two errors in my own mapping table: `Hierarchy` holds its codes in `tree`, and 3.0 `RepresentationMap` has a *second* required choice for the target side. 2.1's `<Report>` layer is the one child mapped to `None` — SDMX 3.0 removed it and sdmxlib follows 3.0.
