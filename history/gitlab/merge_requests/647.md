## Problem
`Attribute.related_measures=()` was stored as given and written as an empty measure relationship, and nothing refused it before the write (`validate()` passed):

| format | written as | valid? |
|---|---|---|
| SDMX-ML 2.1 | nothing (2.1 has no measure relationship) | yes |
| SDMX-ML 3.0 / 3.1 | `<str:MeasureRelationship/>` | no |
| SDMX-JSON | `"measureRelationship": []` | no |

## Why refuse it rather than treat it as unset
**The IM** (SDMX 3.1, Section 2, lines 1402–1408): a `MeasureRelationship` relates an attribute "to one or more Measures", and its absence "defaults to a relationship to all Measures". The schemas agree:
- SDMX-ML 3.0 requires a `<Measure>`.
- SDMX-JSON gives `measureRelationship` `minItems: 1`.

An empty relationship means nothing. Mapping it to `None`, or dropping it on write, would silently widen "no measure" to "every measure".

**The other libraries:**
- **pysdmx 1.20** folds related measures into its attachment string and cannot hold an empty list.
- **sdmx1 2.28** doesn't model which measures at all.

The full comparison is on #497.

## Change
- **`_rules.some_measures`:** a field validator on `Attribute.related_measures`, following #492 stage 1's pattern, since the value alone decides. It refuses `()` on construction and `attrs.evolve`, and `None` keeps meaning every measure.
- **Readers:** they refuse such a DSD per artefact, whether it arrives as an SDMX-JSON `[]` or as an SDMX-ML 3.0 relationship whose `<Measure>` elements are all empty.
- **`test_measure_relationship`:** covers construction, `evolve`, the valid states and both readers. 4 of its 6 tests fail with the validator removed.
- **Upgrade note** in `docs/upgrading.md`.
