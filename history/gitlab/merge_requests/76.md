## What's covered now

### 1. \`role=\"Actual\"\` round-trip on DataConstraint
Per \`ConstraintRoleType\` the only legal values are \`{Allowed, Actual}\` — the audit had wrongly claimed \"Forbidden\" was a third value. We've only ever round-tripped Allowed. Now both roles are exercised.

### 2. \`CubeRegion @include=\"false\"\` round-trip
The exclude-region semantic (what the audit thought was \"Forbidden\") lives on the CubeRegion's include flag, not on the constraint's role.

- A second \`CubeRegion\` with \`include=\"false\"\` on the existing \`CC_TEST\`. Per the \`DataConstraintType\` XSD, \`CubeRegion\` has \`maxOccurs=\"2\"\` — one allowed-region + one excluded-region is the legal shape.
- A second \`DataConstraint\` \`CC_ACTUAL\` with \`role=\"Actual\"\` attached to the same Dataflow. The two roles are semantically distinct: Allowed describes what *can* be reported; Actual describes what *is* reported.
