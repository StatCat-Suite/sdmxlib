`test_the_registered_path_is_dramatically_cheaper` has been red on `main` throughout this milestone. **It was not flaky and not a broken feature — its premise expired.**

## What changed under us

#271 measured DuckDB's Python client at **~48 µs per bound value** and put the crossover between binding and registering at *four* values. On duckdb 1.5.3 binding costs **~1.9 µs** — about 25× cheaper. The test's 5× threshold at 200 values is unreachable, and it was correctly reporting so: the measured gap at 200 is ~1.2×.

Re-measured (median of 7, after warm-up, 200k-row table):

```
    N        bound      registered     ratio
    10       0.85 ms      0.94 ms       0.9x
    100      1.00 ms      0.85 ms       1.2x
    500      1.68 ms      0.81 ms       2.1x
    1000     2.44 ms      0.80 ms       3.0x
    5000     9.75 ms      1.09 ms       8.9x
    20000   35.26 ms      1.55 ms      22.7x
    50000   93.16 ms      2.92 ms      31.9x
```

**The shape survives and is what the feature rests on** — linear against flat, so the gap keeps widening and is 32× by 50,000. **The crossover does not**: it is around 500–1000, not four.

## Why this mattered beyond a red test

The stale figure was steering **user-facing guidance**. `Session.value_set`'s docstring and `docs/cookbook/discovery.md` both told readers to reach for a scoped context manager at four values — where a plain list is now the same speed and simpler. Both corrected.

Four design-doc mentions are kept as history with the rate date-stamped rather than rewritten, since they are recording what #271 found at the time.

## Why the test is deleted, not retuned

`docs/design/caching.md` asks a guard to assert the **count** of expensive calls, not wall-clock. Two tests in this very file already do exactly that — `test_the_values_never_reach_the_parameter_list` and `test_bind_count_does_not_grow_with_the_set` pin the mechanism (one bind instead of N) in a way that cannot expire when a dependency gets faster. The wall-clock test was redundant as a guard and fragile as a measurement.

The module docstring now carries the curve, the duckdb version, and an instruction to re-measure before quoting any of it — these are properties of a dependency, not of this library, and they have already moved by a factor of 25.
