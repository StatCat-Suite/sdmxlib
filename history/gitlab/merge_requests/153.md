Adds a **Serialization** section to `docs/design/performance.md` recording the benchmarked finding from the recent JSON perf work, so the "why not cattrs/msgspec" question is answered for the next person.

Includes the head-to-head table (our dict+orjson 127 µs vs cattrs 272 µs vs orjson-native-dataclass 292 µs vs msgspec 91 µs) and the conclusions: cattrs and native-dataclass are both *slower* than our hand-written emit; msgspec's edge is internal to its Rust pipeline and not reconstructable by combining cattrs/dataclasses with orjson; adopting msgspec means a heavy dep + a separate Struct writer layer, not worth ~15–20% given we beat both peers 2.4–4.4× on parse.
