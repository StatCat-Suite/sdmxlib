mutmut 3.8.0 (allowed by `mutmut>=3.3,<4`) renamed the internals `tools/mutmut_forksafe.py` patched:
- `mutmut._stats` is now `state()._stats`;
- the per-mutant fork loop is now a `MutantRunner`.

Any fresh environment failed `just typecheck` in `test_mutmut_import_time.py`, which is what blocked !624's gate. Local venvs on 3.7 hid it, the same stale-lock pattern as duckdb 1.5.5 vs 1.5.6.

3.8 also ships `process_isolation = "forkserver"`. It keeps pytest out of the forking parent, which is the #314 fix the tool did by hand.

**The tool now:**
- requires forkserver mode (set in `[tool.mutmut]`) and refuses fork mode as #314;
- hands mutmut a `CreditingRunner`, whose stats pass carries the #458 import-time probe and credit. 3.8 still gives collection-time hits to the first test;
- keeps `_preload`. Measured without it, the stats child exits -6 (SIGABRT) loading pyarrow.

**Verified:**
- `gaps.py` against two test files: 622 mutants in 79 s with 4 children;
- `_any_node_has` credited to 34 tests, not 1;
- the 2 timeouts are real infinite loops in `_follow`;
- sabotaging the credit, or the probe, fails its test;
- `just ci` green in a fresh 3.8 environment: 10,805 unit, plus docs, integration and bundle.

The mutmut floor is now 3.8. `docs/design/mutation-evidence.md` records the move.
