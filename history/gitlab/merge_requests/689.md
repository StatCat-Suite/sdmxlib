#508, the attachment half, as decided: **`attachment` is required.**

**Model:**
- `Attribute.attachment` is `field(kw_only=True, validator=attachment_stated)` with no default. It is keyword-only because the fields before it have defaults; the same precedent as `name`.
- `MetadataAttributeUsage.attachment` is required in the same way.
- Leaving it out is a `TypeError` at the call, and a pyrefly finding before that. An explicit `None` (from a pass-through, or a store row) is refused by the new `_rules.attachment_stated` as the field is set.

**Write-time check removed:** `_check_attachment_stated` could no longer fire, so it is deleted. The IM census rule `IM-5.3.2.1-10` moves from `Validated` to `Structural`, citing the validator. That follows the precedent of `version_type` and `named`, and the exact counts move from 78 → 77 checks and 18 → 17 cited.

**Readers:** the wire readers already read a missing `AttributeRelationship` as `DATASET` (#421), so only their annotations tighten. The storage reader refuses a NULL a pre-#508 store may hold, through `_stored_attachment`; `get` raises `SdmxConformanceError ("stored and refused")`, and a bulk read records it in `refused`.

**Fixtures:** 31 hand-built call sites across 21 test files gain an explicit `AttachmentLevel.DATASET`. pyrefly listed every one, and no runtime-only stragglers turned up. The doc examples already stated one, apart from two lines in `upgrading.md`, whose existing bullet is rewritten to the current behaviour.

**Tests** (`test_attachment_required.py`):
- no default, on both classes;
- the validator is on the field and refuses `None`, checked directly rather than by passing `None`, which the checker refuses;
- every level builds (discrimination check);
- a store row with NULL is refused on read.

The old write-time test is removed.
