Closes #262.

`filter()` only ANDed and negation was a `negate` flag on two of thirteen predicate kinds; there was no route to a disjunction at all. `And` / `Or` / `Not` are shared nodes in `_query_common`, so all four DSLs get them from one definition.

**The implementation decision.** They override `to_sql` rather than registering in each DSL's `_EMITTERS` table. That table dispatches on `type(pred)` over a closed set, but `filter()` compiles through `pred.to_sql(ctx)` — the cross-library seam pinaxlib implements — so table dispatch would have made `ours & theirs` fail on something that already worked.

That surfaced two things, both fixed here:

- Only `code_query` compiled through the protocol seam. The other three called `_emit_predicate` directly, so foreign predicates never worked there. All four aligned.
- Those three do not splice `ctx.join_sqls`. Previously unreachable; now a foreign predicate requesting a join raises `NotImplementedError` naming the limit rather than dropping the join and returning wrong rows.

**Precedence guard on `FieldRef.__rand__`/`__ror__`, not only `Predicate`.** `code.id == "A" | code.id == "B"` parses as `code.id == ("A" | code.id)`, so Python falls through to the *ref*. Guarding only the predicate side left the real mistake reporting `unsupported operand type(s) for |: 'str' and '_IdRef'`.

**NULL semantics** are SQL's, documented on `__invert__`: double negation holds, excluded middle fails over a nullable column. Pinned as an example, with the contrast that `~` over an annotation predicate *does* complement (`NOT EXISTS` is total).

**Tests.** Recursive predicate-tree generators in both property files — a flat conjunction cannot find a parenthesisation bug — plus distributivity, absorption, idempotence, De Morgan, and `filter(a).filter(b) == filter(a & b)`. Both oracles gain `and_`/`or_`/`not_`, restated rather than shared, with the argument for why the unknown-to-false collapse commutes with AND and OR but not NOT. 22 examples in `test_query_boolean.py`.

Two judgement calls: `AggPredicate` deliberately gets no combinators (pinned with the reason — `having()` already ANDs, and a disjunction of two aggregates has no caller), and `!=`/`is_not_null` stay as sugar; the issue called either defensible.
