## Summary

Final phase of the local bench harness. 20 benchmarks comparing the elegant navigational API (\`dsd.dimensions[\"FREQ\"].codelist.codes\`) against pre-built dict access on a fully-resolved \`InMemoryRegistry\`. Quantifies the per-op tax we pay for attribute-walk ergonomics so future model-layer optimisations have a baseline.

## Coverage

| Bench | Comparison |
|---|---|
| Single dim lookup | \`ItemList[id]\` vs \`dict[id]\` |
| Single Ref unwrap | \`dim.codelist\` vs \`cl_by_dim_id[id]\` |
| Full traversal | nav comprehension vs pre-built dict |
| High-level API | \`dsd.all_codelists\`, \`dsd.code_map(lang=)\` |
| Repeated access (100×) | catches per-call overhead amortised |

Sizes: 5×50 and 10×200 (dimensions × codes per dim).

## What the numbers say (laptop run)

| Operation | Direct | Nav | Tax |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single dim lookup | 31 ns | 68 ns | **2.2×** |
| Codelist resolve | 31 ns | 66 ns | **2.1×** |
| 100× dim lookup | 1.0 µs | 5.0 µs | **5.0×** |
| Walk all codes (10×200) | 3.1 µs | 17.9 µs | **5.8×** |

Two findings worth noting:

1. **\`ItemList[id]\` is the biggest single target.** 2-3× slower than \`dict[id]\` on a single lookup, and it shows up in every walk. Likely a linear scan over the underlying tuple. A parallel id-to-index dict would be cheap to add and would compound across the entire model.
2. **\`Ref.__call__\` + isinstance check costs ~35 ns per resolve.** Fine at small scale; at thousands of accesses per request it adds up. Less obvious to optimise.
