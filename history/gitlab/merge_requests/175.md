## What changed

- **Dropped `orjson` from baseline deps** (`pyproject.toml`). Verified with orjson *uninstalled*: everything still builds, checks, and passes.
- Swapped the last decode-then-assert helpers in 9 test files + `examples/sink_sdmx_json.py` off `orjson.loads`/`orjson.dumps` → `msgspec.json.decode`/`encode`. (msgspec encodes NaN/inf as JSON `null`, same as orjson's `OPT_SERIALIZE_NUMPY`, so the missing-observation behaviour is preserved — locked by the existing `test_nan_serialises_as_null`.)
- **Docs**: rewrote the `performance.md` "Serialization: dict + orjson, not msgspec / cattrs" section — that decision was revisited in #8 and reversed for **typing + design** (typed DTOs both directions; the `Any` boundary localised to one `msgspec.json.decode`), not raw speed. The measurement table is kept as evidence and now shows the chosen msgspec path is also the fastest of the four. Updated `json-and-parsing.md` and `CLAUDE.md` to point at msgspec.
