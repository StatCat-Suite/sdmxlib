Closes #486.

**The defect.** The IM (§4.3.3.2) says *"The latest Codelist used overrides any previous Codelist."* `Codelist.apply_extensions` let the **earliest** win, and its docstring credited FMR for that. FMR does the opposite.

**The evidence.** Two codelists, X1 and X2, share code A. Each was submitted to the local FMR 12.4.2 and read back as SDMX-ML 2.1, which has no extensions, so FMR flattens them:

| codelist | extends, in order | FMR serves A as | sdmxlib before |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CL_E12 | X1, X2 (own B) | **A from X2**, B own | A from X1 |
| CL_E21 | X2, X1 | **A from X1** | A from X2 |

**The change:**
- `apply_extensions` lets a later extension replace an earlier one's code. Own codes still win, and an inherited code keeps the place it first appeared in.
- The docstring, the field comment and `docs/upgrading.md` (a new section) are updated.
- The census row `IM-4.3.3.2-1` now names the test.

**The tests:**
- `tests/integration/test_fmr_extension_precedence.py` replays the recorded FMR exchange: what was sent is what we send now, our result matches FMR's for both orders, and the recording can tell the two orders apart.
- The unit test now expects the later extension to win.
- Both fail on the old code: 3 failures, checked.
