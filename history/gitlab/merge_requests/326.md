Not a reader that forgot to select the column — there was no column.
The flow's committed dimensions were dropped at the write and read back
as `()`, which reads as "uses the whole DSD". That is precisely the
opposite of what the constraint says, and it is the question catalogue
discovery asks.

Stored as `TEXT[]` on `dataflow` rather than a child table: a set of
ids with no attributes of its own and nothing joining to it, the same
shape as `dsd_component.related_dimensions`. NULL and empty both mean
unconstrained, so a row written before the column existed reads as the
model's default rather than as a loss. SCHEMA_VERSION 2.9 -> 2.10.

This empties KNOWN_LOSSES. The #316 guard opened with sixteen entries
across #320, #322 and #323, and every one was deleted by a fix rather
than by a decision to tolerate it — so the store now round-trips every
field of every persistable artefact, and the guard says so by having
nothing left to say.
