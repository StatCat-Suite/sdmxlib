## Multi-level hierarchies in SDMX-ML 3.x — fixed

The 3.x XSD gives a `Hierarchy` one `Level`, and each `Level` holds the next one down. The writer emitted levels as **siblings** (XSD-invalid past one level) and the reader read siblings, so a conformant document's second level was dropped (the registered `str:Level/str:Level` loss). Both now follow the chain — what the 2.1 writer/reader (`_write_level_chain` / `_read_level_chain`) already did.

- `_v3_writer._write_hierarchy` nests each level in the last; `_v3_reader._level_chain` walks it.
- The `str:Level/str:Level` `LOSSES` row, its injection, and its element-census and JSON-census entries go.
- The maximal fixture gains a second level, with the leaf placed in it, so the field census holds all four formats and the store to it. That surfaced the first level's declared limits (coding format `decimals`/`isMultiLingual`, annotation value/URLs) at the new `tree[].children[].level` site, now declared in `gaps.py` and registered.
- The strict xfail becomes `test_a_hierarchy_of_two_levels_is_a_chain` (written nested, read back as `["L1", "L2"]`).

## SDMX-ML 2.1 regex / index value maps — not a defect

2.1 validation already refuses both (#242: "a regex mapping has no 2.1 form … serialise to 3.0"), and `sl.serialize` runs it. The xfail had called `sdmx_ml21.writer.write` directly, which does not validate. It is now `test_sdmx_ml_2_1_refuses_a_regex_or_index_value_map`, through `serialize`. The variants stay in `tests/support/shapes.py` (3.x and JSON) rather than `maximal`, which the field census writes to 2.1 unvalidated.
