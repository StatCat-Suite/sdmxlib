## Two things found on the way

**The "~100 ms" comment was wrong by an order of magnitude.** Three per-module `_schema` caches carried `"""Module-level cache so each test pays the ~100 ms compile cost once."""`. Measured: 2.1 → 12 ms, 3.0 → 6 ms, 3.1 → 6 ms. Consolidating nine per-module caches into one saves roughly 70 ms across the whole suite. The new docstring says the point is having one definition, not speed — I would rather not ship a rationale I just disproved.

**`tests/` is not typechecked.** `parse()` returns `StructureMessage | MetadataMessage`; every per-module `_load` annotated it `-> StructureMessage` and nothing complained, because `just typecheck` runs `basedpyright src` and `pyrefly.toml` sets `project-includes = ["src"]`. 31k lines of tests, no checker. The shared loader narrows explicitly with an `isinstance` assert. Whether to widen the checkers to `tests/` is a real question with a real cost — it belongs to #212, which is where the coverage-and-audit verdicts live.

## Verification

**No assertion semantics changed.** All 18 touched `assert` lines are call-site renames with an identical predicate (`_load("minimal")` → `load_xml("2.1", "minimal")`).

**The harness still does its job.** The whole reason `roundtrip_xml` exists is the XSD validation step — a plain round-trip cannot catch a writer emitting non-conformant XML that our own reader tolerates, since both share the blind spot. A consolidation that silently dropped that step would look identical and pass. So I mutated `WriterHelpers.write_codelist` to emit a bogus child element: **66 of these tests fail**. The validation survived the move.

`just ci` green — 2511 unit, 72 docs.

This pass removes 143 lines from `tests/unit/` and adds 176 in `tests/support/`, so it is **+33 net** — and the shared modules are the *right* place for docstrings explaining why the harness is shaped as it is. That gate would push toward under-documenting shared infrastructure to move a number. Duplicate-definition count (100 → 68) is the metric that actually tracks the goal; I will amend the issue.
