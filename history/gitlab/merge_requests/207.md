`src/` now carries **one** complexity suppression, and it is the only one that ever explained itself.

## write_text_format — a table written as control flow

Eleven independent `if x is not None: el.set(...)` statements. Now an ordered `dict[str, str | None]` of xml-name → formatted-value, so each `Facet` field sits on one line beside the attribute it emits. The risk the linter was pointing at is concrete: a `Facet` field added and not mirrored here is silent data loss on write that XSD validation cannot catch. Emission order preserved exactly.

Two module-level formatters carry the type-specific rendering. `_xsd_bool` is keyword-only rather than suppressing `FBT001` for one helper.

## read() in ml30/ml31 — not dispatch

The whole branch count came from five mechanical loops:

```python
for cl in codelists:
    _helpers.index_artefact(index, cl, Codelist)
```

A new `ReaderHelpers.index_all` collapses each to one call. Applied to **ml21** too, which sat just under the threshold and so was never on the list — the shape was identical, it just had fewer artefact types.

## _resolve_urns — three jobs

A partition plus two unrelated load passes sharing only `memo`. Split into `_partition_urns`, `_resolve_scheme_items`, `_resolve_top_urns`, each with a docstring saying what an absent memo entry means.

## _emit_predicate — kept

Exhaustive dispatch over a closed sum type; eleven arms, ten of them one-line delegations. The docstring now records why both mechanical alternatives are worse: a `dict[type, emitter]` table needs a uniform signature, but only three of eleven emitters take the join arguments — the other eight would carry three dead parameters each; and moving emission onto the predicate classes puts SQL generation inside the DSL's data structs.
