## Forward-compat audit (\`tests/unit/test_xsd_schemas.py\`)

The audit runs each of our 3.0 fixtures through the 3.1 XSD after a \`v3_0\` → \`v3_1\` namespace rewrite. Findings:

- ✅ \`with_groups.xml\` — DSD / Group / Measure shapes carried over cleanly.
- ✅ \`with_metadata.xml\` — MSD + Metadataflow shapes from MR !61 carried over cleanly.
- ⚠️ \`minimal.xml\` (and the others) — diverges on exactly two attributes that 3.1 removed:
  - \`Categorisation/@version\` — 3.1 dropped versioning on Categorisation (now sui-generis like AgencyScheme in 3.0). Tracked as **#103**.
  - \`DataConstraint/@role\` — 3.1 removed the \`role=\` attribute; the Allowed/Forbidden semantic was reworked. Tracked as **#104**.

The divergence set is pinned by \`test_minimal_30_diverges_from_31_on_known_attrs\`: a future upstream change surfaces here so the 3.1 implementer gets a clear signal.
