## Fixture rationale

Only \`tests/fixtures/sdmx_ml21/ecb_data_structure.xml\` round-trips through all three libraries. The others are excluded for legitimate parser-coverage reasons:

- \`minimal.xml\`, \`with_hierarchy.xml\` — sdmx1 doesn't implement \`<str:ReleaseCalendar>\`.
- \`ecb_dataflow.xml\` — pysdmx rejects \`<ContentConstraint type=…>\`.
- \`complete.xml\` — same as \`with_hierarchy.xml\`.

Synthetic-fixture comparison was on the table but pysdmx also rejects our synthetic-message output (XSD validation issue surfaced under their reader), so synthetic remains in \`test_parse.py\` / \`test_serialize.py\` for our own regression tracking.
